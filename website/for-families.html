For families

The family is the buying unit.

LINK is for households that already earn across everyday programs — and want those points to become something they can enjoy together.

Gen Y (30–44) and Gen X (45+) are the primary value contributors and decision-makers. Gen Z (18–29) is the activator who often installs, invites, and nudges the household into motion.

Worth More Together.

Three roles. One pool.

Nan, 42 — The Value Accumulator Primary decision
“Show me that the family gets real value — and let me approve what moves.”

Working mother and household decision-maker. High family spend, value-driven. She needs proof that pooling protects value — and explicit consent before points move.

Wit, 54 — The Trusted Provider Primary value
“I earn points. I just don’t have time to chase them across five apps.”

Strong purchasing power, careful spender. He earns consistently but tracks points less actively. He needs security reassurance and a tangible family payoff.

Ploy, 24 — The Activator Setup & admin
“I’ll set it up. Just make it simple enough that everyone can join.”

Digital-native and phone-first. She opens the family pool, links programs, and nudges expiring points — removing setup friction so Nan and Wit can decide with confidence.

The payoff

The reward is the table everyone sits at.

A pooled goal is easier to care about than a balance. LINK keeps the target something the household can picture — a meal, a trip, a weekend that belongs to all of them.
