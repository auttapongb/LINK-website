What business?

Not a loyalty wallet. Household progress.

Functional (too small)

“We aggregate points.”

True — and forgettable. Any fintech clone can claim a dashboard. This frame invites feature wars.

Brand (the real business)

We turn scattered family loyalty into a shared getaway.

Like Nestlé → health & wellness, LINK reframes from utility to outcome: families that move together, worth more together.

Commercial model (Year 1): free to households. LINK monetises via partner transfer / settlement fees and aggregate lift reporting — not consumer subscriptions, not advertising against raw household ledgers.

Four competitor types

The ones you don’t see kill you.

Taxonomy without names is theatre. Named Thai / regional rivals — with where LINK wins and where it dies.

Direct

The1 · AIS Points · partner-native apps

Win: cross-program household pool + shared getaway goal.
Die if: we look like another siloed balance screen.

Adjacent

GrabRewards · TrueMoney / bank super-apps

Win: loyalty-points job, not payments; privacy-first consent UX.
Die if: they bolt on family pooling inside a wallet already trusted daily.

Substitute

Cash piggy-bank · Agoda / Booking promos · LINE travel

Win: unlock value families already earned — lower perceived cash pain.
Die if: OTA deals feel simpler than linking five programs.

Emergent

IHG One Rewards family tools · AI “move my points” agents

Win: multi-earn rails into one burn — hotel alone cannot own grocery/BTS.
Die if: a partner coalition ships household pooling without LINK.

Deep teardown · GrabRewards (12-month copy risk)

What Grab already owns

  • Daily habit (ride / food) + wallet trust
  • Points earned inside their checkout
  • Push + in-app real estate at massive scale

What Grab will not easily own

  • Lotus’s grocery ledger + BTS Rabbit + AIS + IHG in one neutral pool
  • Political willingness of rivals to surface inside Grab
  • Explicit multi-adult consent UX positioned as family fairness

12-month scenario: Grab ships “Family points” for Grab-only earn. LINK still wins if the job is cross-coalition getaway math. LINK dies if we only optimise Grab-like checkout rewards — that is their game.

Deep teardown · The1 / Central retail stack

What The1 already owns

  • Deep grocery / retail earn inside Lotus’s and Central formats
  • Member habit at checkout — highest-frequency Thai loyalty rail
  • Ability to bundle in-ecosystem rewards without LINK

Where LINK still differentiates

  • Neutral pool that can include AIS + BTS + IHG without Central owning the graph
  • Household consent / fairness UX The1 apps treat as secondary
  • Getaway burn narrative vs more retail redemption gravity

Partner posture: treat The1 as MoU #1 earn rail candidate — never as a UI war. If Central ships household pooling across only its banners, LINK must stay the open multi-coalition layer or lose the job.

Why partners won’t just ship this

The moat is the household graph — with teeth.

Multi-rail neutrality

Lotus’s will not happily surface AIS balances inside its app. IHG will not operate BTS Rabbit. LINK is the Switzerland that makes pooling politically possible.

Consent switching costs

Once a household has linked programs, set a goal, and built contribution history, re-creating that graph elsewhere is painful — if trust stays intact.

Coalition threat (honest)

If IHG + Central Retail + a bank form a closed family pool, LINK dies. Mitigation: stay useful as the open layer; price transfer fees so partners prefer LINK to building.

What is not a moat

UI polish, “AI”, or a prettier progress bar. Those are table stakes Grab can copy in a quarter.
