Partners

Earn where life already happens.

LINK’s Year-1 pilot concept focuses on five everyday programs. Partner names are used for academic illustration only.

Lotus’s

Household shopping turns everyday spend into progress. Groceries become visible contributions instead of forgotten leftovers.

BTS

The commute becomes a steady contribution to the shared goal — small rides that add up when the family can finally see them together.

AIS

Recurring telecom spend helps the pool grow in the background, without asking anyone to become a points hobbyist.

iBerry

Family meals and social moments can contribute to the goal — the kind of spending that already happens when people gather.

IHG

The Year-1 hero destination: turn the family’s progress into a getaway. Public positioning focuses on this shared trip outcome.
