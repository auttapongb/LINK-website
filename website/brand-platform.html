Why partners won’t just ship this

The moat is the household graph — with teeth.

Multi-rail neutrality

Lotus’s will not happily surface AIS balances inside its app. IHG will not operate BTS Rabbit. LINK is the Switzerland that makes pooling politically possible.

Consent switching costs

Once a household has linked programs, set a goal, and built contribution history, re-creating that graph elsewhere is painful — if trust stays intact.

Coalition threat (honest)

If IHG + Central Retail + a bank form a closed family pool, LINK dies. Mitigation: stay useful as the open layer; price transfer fees so partners prefer LINK to building.

What is not a moat

UI polish, “AI”, or a prettier progress bar. Those are table stakes Grab can copy in a quarter.
