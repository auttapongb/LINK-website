Privacy & consent

Your family shares the goal — not control of each other’s points.

LINK is designed around a privacy-first model with on-device visibility, tokenized connections and explicit approval before points move.

On-device visibility

Point information is brought into a shared experience without turning the family pool into a free-for-all.

Explicit consent

Each member stays in control of point movement — nothing transfers without approval.

Tokenized connections

Partner links are designed to stay minimal — enough to see opportunity, not to surrender ownership.

FAQ

Straight answers before you explore further.

What is LINK?

LINK is a loyalty-point aggregation and family pooling service. It helps a household see points across everyday programs and move them toward a shared goal — with consent.

Who can create a family pool?

Any household member can start a pool. In practice, a Gen Z activator often sets it up, while Gen Y/X members contribute the most value and approve transfers.

Can one person move another person’s points without approval?

No. Transfers require explicit confirmation from the member whose points are moving.

Which programs are included in the Year-1 pilot?

The illustrative pilot focuses on BTS, Lotus’s, AIS, iBerry and IHG.

Is this a live commercial service?

No. This website presents an academic/fictional pilot concept for illustration. It is not a live signup for a launched consumer product.
