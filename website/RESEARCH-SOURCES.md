## Methodology statement (for slides / professor FAQ)

1. **Evidence type:** secondary / desk research only for this pilot concept.
2. **Not claimed:** attributed n=8–12 household field interviews, ethnography, or proprietary tracker panels.
3. **Workshop vignettes** on Consumer insight remain labelled as synthesised teaching scenarios grounded in secondary patterns — not field transcripts.
4. **Rounding:** large public figures may appear as rounded hero numbers on the page (e.g. “US$263B”); exact source wording lives here.
5. **Conflicts:** when sources disagree (e.g. older The 1 “17M” vs later “20M” press), the page prefers the more recent attributable figure and notes vintage in the citation strip.
