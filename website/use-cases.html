Use cases

Built for people who already do life together.

LINK starts with the Thai household — then extends to other groups that already share routines, meals, or plans.

  • Families See BTS, Lotus’s, AIS, iBerry and IHG balances in one place. Pool toward a getaway with consent from each member. Best fit
  • Roommates / best friends Share progress for dinners out, weekend escapes, or a treat nobody wants to fund alone. Strong fit
  • Food lovers / dining groups Turn social meals into momentum toward a shared celebration or short stay. Strong fit
  • Travel groups Collect toward a stay when companions already plan together. Exploratory in Year-1. Emerging use case
  • Colleagues / commuter groups Lightweight pooling for people who share a commute rhythm more than a household budget. Emerging use case
  • Students / new graduates Smaller balances and longer horizons — a longer-term fit as the platform grows. Emerging use case

Fit labels are qualitative for this pilot concept — not star ratings or survey scores.
