Data strategy

From everyday touchpoints to a family CDP.

LINK does not invent a new loyalty currency. It unifies signals the household already creates — then structures them into consent-aware profiles a Customer Data Platform can activate. This page is written for academic evaluation: clear journey, no live PII claims.

The classic CDP loop, mapped onto LINK’s household loyalty context. Year 1 practices the loop with freemium product data; Year 2 exposes partner-safe outputs.

5 · Privacy & governance

Academic pilot. No real PII harvest.

This website is a fictional / academic Year-1 concept. Numbers and flows are illustrative. Privacy is the brand platform — not a legal guarantee of PDPA compliance.

Granular consent

Per-partner sharing toggles. One-click revoke for future data. Historical ledger integrity does not require keeping marketing permissions forever.

Minimisation

Collect only what the shared goal needs. Prefer tokens over identifiers. Prefer aggregate partner insight over household-level exports.

Composable stance

Query in place where possible. Thailand-context PDPA awareness. Site analytics stay off until a real measurement ID is configured.
