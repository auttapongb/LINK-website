1 · Objectives

Start with objectives — not channels.

Business

Prove a Year-1 pilot: Thai households pool everyday loyalty toward an IHG getaway goal — free to consumers; economics with partners.

Marketing

~60% acquisition of activator households · ~40% retention / habit once a pool is live. Owned channels do the loyalty work.

What we will not do

No fake bank / e-wallet claims. No “zero expiry” promises. No shotgun media before the product story hangs together.

Who pays LINK: Year-1 revenue = partner transfer / settlement fees + aggregate lift packs (anonymised). Households pay THB 0. We refuse ad-tech resale of raw family ledgers — that would contradict the privacy platform.

2 · STP

Segment · Target · Position.

Buying unit = the household

  1. S

    Segment

    Urban Thai households with 2+ loyalty programs and a shared travel / getaway aspiration.

  2. T

    Target

    Primary: Gen Y/X decision-makers & value contributors. Secondary: Gen Z activators who install and admin.

  3. P

    Position

    Worth More Together — the only household layer that makes scattered points useful before they expire alone.

  4. ≠

    Not this

    Not a new points currency. Not a super-app. Not “better rewards” — a different system for shared progress.

Problem → Promise → Action.

Promise

Thai: รวมกันคุ้มกว่า. Shared visibility + consented pooling toward a real getaway.

Action

Pool them. Take the trip.

Connect programs → pool with consent → burn toward Year-1 IHG hero goal.

4 · Digital mix

Product is the system. Price is free. Place is the phone.

Product / service

  • Household pool visibility across BTS, Lotus’s, AIS, iBerry, IHG
  • Explicit consent before any transfer
  • Earn → Pool → Burn loop with Year-1 IHG getaway as hero burn

Price / value

  • Free to the household in the pilot model
  • Economics sit with partner-side transfers / lift
  • No consumer subscription framing on public pages

Place / distribution

  • Smartphone as watering hole — activator installs for the family
  • Partner touchpoints seed awareness (QR / in-app prompts — conceptual)
  • This marketing site as owned proof surface for evaluators

Promotion / people / process

  • Campaign line + demo as conversion path
  • Roles: Nan (decide), Wit (value), Ploy (activate)
  • Process: See → Think → Onboard → Activate → Habit → Complete → Advocate

5 · Channels (PESO)

Omni-channel around the household journey.

Customers do not think in channels. LINK plans touchpoints against the journey — with conscious choices about what we will not fund in Year 1.

Paid · ~25% Year-1 media

Meta / TikTok / LINE ads to activators (18–29) + retarget decision-makers. Kill-list: TV, OOH, broad performance until pool-start ≥ target.

Earned · planned moments

Q2 campus EMBA critique thread; Q4 first getaway completion kit for partner PR; professor / industry lunch-and-learn — zero fabricated testimonials. Owner: Marketing.

Shared · LINE-native

LINE OA rich menu: Check pool · Invite family · Expiry alert. Activator Stories template (“พาครอบครัวไปเที่ยวด้วยแต้ม”). Weekly sticker progress — not a discount dump.

Owned · ~75% effort

Site, demo, app alerts, expiry nudges, pool rituals. Where measurement and retention live.

6 · Loyalty system

Products compete. Systems scale.

LINK is not a better points balance — it is a household system that builds habit (Luckin-style friction removal) while protecting consent (privacy-first bonds).

Earn

Everyday partners

BTS · Lotus’s · AIS · iBerry feed the pool through normal life.

Pool

Consented transfer

Each member confirms. Shared goal makes contribution visible and fair.

Burn

IHG getaway

Year-1 hero redemption. Phase-2 may add micro-burns — never contradict the hero goal.

8 · Pilot timeline

Twelve months to prove the system.

Q1 · Foundation

Story + demo + partner map

Ship this site, interactive demo, privacy stance, academic plan pages. Align Year-1 partner set.

Q2 · Soft launch

Activator acquisition

Campus / urban Gen Z seed. Owned onboarding. Instrument consent + pool starts.

Q3 · Habit

Household retention

Expiry alerts, contribution nudges, mid-year progress rituals. Tune acquisition vs retention spend.

Q4 · Proof

First getaway completions

Document earn→burn case studies. Brief Year-2 CDP activation with partners.
