Secondary insight pack (desk · Aug 2026)

What we already know without pretending fieldwork is done

  • Fragmented rails: public program pages for The1 / Lotus’s, AIS Points, BTS Rabbit, IHG One Rewards show separate balances and non-trivial transfer rules — “just merge it” is not a consumer default.
  • LINE as coordination layer: Thailand’s dominant chat OS for family admin; onboarding briefs assume LINE invite, not email.
  • OTA mental math: Agoda/Booking promo culture trains cash getaways — LINK must beat that with a visible pool progress bar, not another points FAQ.
  • Competitor walkthroughs (desk): GrabRewards and partner-native apps optimise single-ecosystem earn; none present multi-adult consent toward a hotel burn as the hero job.
  • Remaining field gate: replace workshop vignettes with attributed n=8–12 paired interview findings when the research sprint completes.

Customer journey

Not linear — still must be planned.

  1. 1

    Awareness

    Expiry anxiety / “we never use points.” Paid + shared seed; owned site proves the story.

  2. 2

    Consideration

    Demo walkthrough. Nan checks consent. Wit checks partner fit. Extensive problem-solving.

  3. 3

    Onboard

    Ploy installs, invites household, links programs. Friction here is fatal.

  4. 4

    Activate

    First consented pool transfer. Habit begins — alerts, progress, rituals.

  5. 5

    Complete

    IHG-ready threshold. Getaway booked. Emotional payoff locks the brand.

  6. 6

    Advocate

    Family story shared. High-loyalty / mid-value members become earned media.

Loyalty bonds

Financial · social · structural.

Financial

Pooled value toward a tangible stay. Clear progress bar beats abstract “miles.”

Social

Household visibility creates peer pressure inside the family — contribution is seen.

Structural

Linked programs + consent history raise switching cost — carefully, with trust.

Value × loyalty matrix (treat differently)

High value · high loyalty

Protect. Early access to getaway inventory. Recognition, not spam discounts.

High value · low loyalty

Entice frequency — smart nudges tied to their earn pattern (weather, commute, basket).

Low value · high loyalty

Drive advocacy and bring-a-cousin invites. Emotional fans amplify earned.

Low · low

Do not overspend. Light automation only — strategy is also what you will not do.
