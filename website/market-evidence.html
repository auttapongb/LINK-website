Implication · Loyalty & data maturity

Privacy paradox: low worry, low control — consent must be visible.

Consent is a product event — not a footer checkbox. Data strategy

What consumers say

  • 87% say they understand data privacy (IAB SEA+India)
  • 75% feel they lack control online (Telenor × GWI 2024)
  • Only ≈1 in 4 worry how companies use their data — lowest in SEA sample
  • 66% will share interest data for free content

What LINK designs for

  • Explicit pool consent — fairness is the trust moment for Nan
  • On-device visibility before partner activation
  • Year-1 freemium data → Year-2 CDP activation (aggregate, consent-bound)
  • SEA CDP demand is rising with privacy enforcement — directional industry trend, not a GMV claim
