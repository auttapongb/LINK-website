Privacy

Your family shares the goal — not control of each other’s points.

Designed around on-device visibility, tokenized connections and explicit consent. This is a privacy-first product stance — not a legal guarantee.

On-device visibility

Point information is brought into a shared experience so the household can see opportunity — without turning the pool into unrestricted access to everyone’s balances.

Explicit consent before transfer

Each member stays in control of point movement. A contribution only proceeds after the owner confirms.

Minimal / tokenized connections

Partner connections are intended to stay limited to what the shared experience needs — not a free harvest of household data.

What this page does not claim

Is LINK “100% secure” or “unhackable”?

No. Those claims are not credible for any consumer service. LINK’s public stance is privacy-first design, not absolute security language.

Does on-device architecture automatically mean PDPA compliance?

No. Architecture can support a careful privacy approach, but legal compliance is not automatic. This site uses cautious wording on purpose.

Is LINK a bank or e-wallet?

No. LINK is positioned as a loyalty display and pooling service — not e-money and not a bank.
