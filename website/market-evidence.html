Implication · Market size / opportunity

A mature digital economy still wastes loyalty.

SEA is past “will people go digital?” — the open job is stitching value that already exists across rails. Thailand is large enough to pilot, dense enough in earn partners, and travel-led enough for an IHG-style burn to feel real.

Implication · Loyalty & data maturity

Privacy paradox: low worry, low control — consent must be visible.

Thailand’s PDPA plus a convenience-first culture is why LINK treats consent as a product event, not a footer checkbox. See Data strategy for the CDP arc.

What consumers say

  • 87% say they understand data privacy (IAB SEA+India)
  • 75% feel they lack control online (Telenor × GWI 2024)
  • Only ≈1 in 4 worry how companies use their data — lowest in SEA sample
  • 66% will share interest data for free content

What LINK designs for

  • Explicit pool consent — fairness is the trust moment for Nan
  • On-device visibility before partner activation
  • Year-1 freemium data → Year-2 CDP activation (aggregate, consent-bound)
  • SEA CDP demand is rising with privacy enforcement — directional industry trend, not a GMV claim
Competitive realism (desk · public T&Cs)

Single-ecosystem apps optimise their own earn — not multi-adult hotel burn

Public walkthroughs of GrabRewards and partner-native apps show strong within-rail loops. None present multi-adult household consent toward a shared hotel redemption as the hero job. That gap is LINK’s positioning — see Brand platform competitor types.

Implication · Measurement for the pilot

Pilot KPIs that match the evidence — not vanity.

Secondary research sets the opportunity; the pilot must prove household activation under consent. Instrumentation stays privacy-real (consent-gated analytics stubs).

Activation

% households that complete first consented pool transfer within 14 days of activator invite — proxy for “LINE night” friction hypothesis.

Multi-rail link rate

Share of pools with ≥2 earn programs linked (BTS / Lotus’s / AIS). Tests whether the fragmentation thesis is felt in behaviour.

Burn intent

Households that set or hit an IHG-ready threshold vs. voucher redemptions — protects the emotional job from Grab-voucher gravity.
